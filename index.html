Balanced 60-question screening comparison over a shared LongMemEval subset — not a complete 500-question reproduction or a universal provider ranking. Providers are pinned to immutable releases and source commits.

Scope

Questions, model roles, answer/judge policy, and comparison settings are fixed before execution. Providers keep their native storage and indexing behaviour. Retries, failures, partial ingestion, costs, and unavailable measurements stay visible, and there is no universal combined score. Retrieval permits query embedding but disables generative query rewriting, reflection, and reranking.
